Graph-QMF~\cite{narang2012graphqmf}
& Two-channel spectral QMF with graph down/up-sampling
& Critical sampling, bipartite spectral folding, alias cancellation, and perfect-reconstruction/orthogonality conditions
& Prior perfect-reconstruction bank; unlike GBDN's redundant, nonsubsampled complete stack \\
Undecimated graph framelets~\cite{zheng2021framelets}
& Full-resolution low/high multiscale framelet coefficients
& Tight-frame decomposition and reconstruction; information-conserving multiscale representation
& Closest tight-stack precedent; filters are not parameterized by learned Blaschke roots \\
Polynomial response filters~\cite{defferrard2016chebnet,he2022chebnetii,he2021bernnet}
& Chebyshev recurrence/interpolation or Bernstein coefficients
& Sparse polynomial evaluation and flexible learned amplitude responses
& Matched controls for the finite-order realization and response-fitting efficiency \\
Adaptive heterophily filters~\cite{chien2021gprgnn,huang2024unifilter,zhang2025herofilter}
& Signed propagation weights, adaptive polynomial bases, or adaptive filter mixing
& Data-dependent responses across homophilic and heterophilic regimes
& Precludes priority claims based only on adaptive spectral learning or heterophily \\
CayleyNets~\cite{levie2019cayleynets}
& Complex Cayley powers with learned coefficients and shared scale $h$
& Rational filtering with a learned, restricted shared pole locus; sparse rational iteration
& Closest pole-family comparator; GBDN independently moves generic exact-target poles \\
SLOG~\cite{xu2024slog}
& Real-valued adaptive non-polynomial filter with subgraph sampling
& Geometrically interpreted, inductive spectral filtering
& Blocks ``first non-polynomial spectral GNN''; a distinct non-polynomial family \\
WaveGC~\cite{liu2025wavegc}
& Multiresolution spectral bases, matrix-valued kernels, and odd/even Chebyshev decomposition
& Wavelet admissibility and short-/long-range analysis
& Multiresolution comparator without Blaschke all-pass root geometry \\
Unitary/stable dynamics~\cite{kiani2024unitary,gravina2023adgn,hariri2025stablechebnet}
& Unitary, antisymmetric, or stable Chebyshev state propagation
& Oversmoothing, stability, non-dissipation, or long-range results for network states/dynamics
& Different object from isometry of GBDN's complete coefficient map \\
Tight GBDN
& Blaschke factors after the Cayley map and complementary $I\!\pm\!\Tcal$ channels
& Exact complete-map isometry and analysis--synthesis; approximate sparse realization
& Proposed combination of independent generic-pole targets and a nonsubsampled tight stack \\
Product-sum GBDN / GBDN+
& Cumulative all-pass sum / all-pass branch with polynomial correction
& Unitary exact factors only / no strict all-pass guarantee
& Expressive relaxations without Tight GBDN's general reconstruction theorem \\
