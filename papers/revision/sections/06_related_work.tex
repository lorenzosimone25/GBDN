Polynomial graph filters learn direct amplitude responses through coefficients of a Laplacian polynomial. ChebNet realizes localized filters with a Chebyshev recurrence~\cite{defferrard2016chebnet}, while ChebNetII replaces unconstrained coefficient learning with Chebyshev interpolation~\cite{he2022chebnetii}. CayleyNets instead use complex rational Cayley polynomials with learned coefficients and a learned scale shared by the powers of one scalar filter~\cite{levie2019cayleynets}. GBDN does not claim polynomial or complex rational filtering as new. Its candidate distinction is independently parameterized Blaschke all-pass roots, their generic exact target-pole geometry, complementary phase-to-amplitude channels, and a reconstructing complete multilevel coefficient map. Whether that parameterization offers a useful finite-order inductive bias is an experimental question.

Stable-ChebNet casts high-order Chebyshev propagation as a stable, non-dissipative dynamical system~\cite{hariri2025stablechebnet}; A-DGN derives stability and non-dissipation from antisymmetric graph dynamics~\cite{gravina2023adgn}. These guarantees concern different objects from tight coefficient analysis. GBDN therefore separates factor unitarity, carried-path contraction, complete coefficient-map isometry, and nonlinear optimization behavior.

Graph wavelet methods provide multiresolution spectral constructions: diffusion scattering builds stable graph representations from diffusion wavelets~\cite{gama2019diffusion}, whereas WaveGC learns admissible graph wavelets by separating odd and even Chebyshev orders~\cite{liu2025wavegc}. Neither citation is used to assert the exact perfect-reconstruction theorem proved here. Classical PDU~\cite{coifman2025pdu} and BDN~\cite{zhang2025bdn} also use Blaschke geometry, but include analytic-signal, inner--outer, selection, or product-sum machinery not supplied by an undirected Laplacian alone. Product-sum GBDN is consequently BDN-inspired rather than equivalent; the cited BDN record is explicitly a withdrawn ICLR submission.

For evaluation, the heterophily suite and its fixed splits follow the audit of Platonov et al.~\cite{platonov2023heterophily}. LRGB~\cite{dwivedi2022lrgb} is reserved for a separate graph-level pipeline; GraphGPS~\cite{rampasek2022graphgps} supplies the upstream framework used by the official WaveGC implementation. These datasets motivate tests, not claims that GBDN already resolves long-range propagation.
